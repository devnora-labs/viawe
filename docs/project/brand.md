# Via-We Brand System

## Purpose
This document defines how VIA-WE Services Pvt. Ltd. must look, feel, speak, and behave across the website and future digital products. It is the shared design authority for Vamshi, Bharath, designers, developers, and AI coding agents.

## Approved identity
- Public brand name: **VIA-WE Services Pvt. Ltd.**
- Tagline: **YOUR DREAMS OUR AIM**
- `360` is a strategic idea only. Never add it to the legal name, logo, wordmark, or tagline.
- Never redesign or distort the approved logo without written approval.

## Positioning
Via-We is a connected business-growth and execution company. It helps businesses move from idea to setup, identity, digital presence, marketing, growth, and expansion through one coordinated relationship.

## Brand personality
Via-We should feel calm, premium, confident, human, strategic, modern, credible, and connected. It must not feel loud, generic, trend-dependent, or AI-generated.

## Core visual idea — The Line That Does Not Drop
The logo contains the brand's visual language:
- one curve represents growth and direction;
- one curve represents partnership and connection;
- the mesh represents relationships and systems;
- the meeting point represents coordinated execution.

This language may guide the hero, section transitions, process diagrams, service relationships, CTA states, and footer. It must never become a decorative squiggle.

## Color system
| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| Ink | `#0B1826` | Primary text and dark sections |
| Brand Deep | `#1B4B77` | Core brand identity and primary CTA |
| Brand Sea | `#3582AD` | Secondary brand accent and connectors |
| Brand Cyan | `#4AD3F3` | Mesh, connection, and signal accent |
| Paper | `#F7FAFC` | Main background |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Clean surfaces and reversed content |

Rules:
- Use official blues as the anchor.
- Do not introduce vermilion, acid green, neon purple, or unrelated accents.
- No gradient text.
- The official logo may preserve its approved internal gradients.
- Cyan is a signal, not a default fill.
- All text pairings must meet WCAG contrast requirements.

## Typography
Use a deliberately selected and properly licensed type system.
- Display: editorial, confident, responsive, tightly controlled.
- Body: readable, 60–75 characters per line, comfortable line height.
- Utility: navigation, captions, labels, metadata.

Rules:
- Do not embed unlicensed fonts.
- Do not treat a temporary font as final.
- Avoid decorative eyebrow labels above every heading.
- Use a limited type scale with responsive display caps.

## Layout
Use authored editorial composition, controlled whitespace, intentional asymmetry, clear chapter changes, and one primary visual voice per viewport.

Avoid:
- repeated three-card grids;
- excessive rounded boxes;
- repeated numbered decorations;
- identical section layouts;
- centered hero plus two equal CTAs;
- floating blobs;
- glassmorphism;
- arbitrary shadows.

## Photography
Photography is proof, not decoration.

Preferred subjects:
- Vijay;
- real Via-We team;
- real office;
- client conversations;
- campaign work;
- website and app reviews;
- whiteboards;
- planning and strategy;
- Indian business context.

Every image must answer: **What does this prove?**

Avoid generic Western stock imagery, posed corporate handshakes, fake teams, and decorative office shots.

## Logo usage
- Use only approved assets.
- Never stretch, compress, or recreate the logo in CSS.
- Do not add `360`.
- Do not place a white-backed raster asset on incompatible surfaces.
- A separately created motion SVG must be labeled as a **web motion asset**, not an official vector master.

## UI treatment
- One clear primary CTA.
- Calm hover and press feedback.
- Visible keyboard focus.
- Full accessible tap targets.
- One coherent elevation system.
- Use cards only when containment is meaningful.
- Keep navigation simple and high contrast.

## Motion personality
Motion must feel precise, calm, connected, intentional, and premium. It must not feel bouncy, elastic, flashy, or like an After Effects template.

## Brand voice
Write with direct verbs and plain English:
- build;
- plan;
- connect;
- launch;
- execute;
- grow;
- expand.

Avoid:
- "unlock your potential";
- "seamless solutions";
- "next-level";
- "innovative solutions";
- "in today's fast-paced world".

## Never allowed
- fake client logos;
- fake testimonials;
- fake metrics;
- fabricated awards;
- neon;
- glassmorphism;
- random gradients;
- particles;
- cyber styling;
- unrelated 3D objects;
- unapproved services;
- generic AI-agency copy.

## Brand approval checklist
1. Could this belong to another agency?
2. Is the connected-system idea visible?
3. Is the logo philosophy respected?
4. Is the page clear within five seconds?
5. Does it feel credible for Indian businesses?
6. Is the motion meaningful?
7. Is the content supported?
8. Is it accessible?
9. Is the typography intentional?
10. Can the team maintain it?
