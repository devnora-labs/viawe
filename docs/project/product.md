# Via-We Website Product Definition

## Purpose
The Via-We website is the official digital business presence for VIA-WE Services Pvt. Ltd. It must:
1. explain the company clearly;
2. generate qualified conversations;
3. demonstrate Via-We's ability to deliver premium websites, branding, marketing, and digital experiences.

It is both a commercial website and a flagship proof of work.

## Product vision
Build a premium, animated, production-ready website that shows how Via-We connects important stages of business growth through one coordinated relationship.

The website must feel like a guided business journey, not a brochure or generic agency portfolio.

## Current active service scope
1. Business Setup
2. Branding & Digital Marketing
3. Website Development
4. Mobile App Development
5. Franchise Solutions

Hiring and recruitment are intentionally excluded from the current website scope.

## Core product message
> Via-We helps businesses start, build, market, digitize, grow, and expand through one connected execution system.

## Target audiences
- entrepreneurs starting a business;
- SMEs and established local businesses;
- brands needing stronger positioning and marketing;
- companies needing websites or mobile applications;
- businesses planning franchise expansion;
- founders seeking one coordinated execution partner.

## Primary user journey
```text
Arrival
→ Understand Via-We
→ Recognize the connected service model
→ See how the process works
→ Build trust
→ Choose a relevant service
→ Start a WhatsApp or enquiry conversation
```

## Homepage narrative
The homepage should work as six connected chapters:

1. **Arrive** — establish identity and promise.
2. **The System** — show the services as one ecosystem.
3. **The Belief** — explain why Via-We coordinates the work.
4. **The Road** — show the journey from idea to expansion.
5. **The Rhythm** — explain how Via-We operates.
6. **Begin** — create a clear path to conversation.

## Recommended Version 1 routes
```text
/
 /services
 /services/business-setup
 /services/branding-digital-marketing
 /services/web-development
 /services/mobile-app-development
 /services/franchise-solutions
 /about
 /contact
 /privacy
 /terms
```

Add `/work` only when public-ready proof exists. Do not publish empty routes.

## Conversion strategy
Primary conversion:
- WhatsApp conversation.

Secondary conversion:
- enquiry form, after destination and routing are approved.

Use no more than three major asks:
1. hero CTA;
2. contextual mid-page CTA;
3. final CTA.

## Trust strategy
Trust must come from:
- real office and team photography;
- project screenshots;
- approved case studies;
- verified process;
- public-ready testimonials;
- clear contact information.

Until case studies are approved, show proof-of-method, such as what week one looks like, how approvals work, and how reporting is handled.

## Product requirements
The site must be:
- responsive;
- mobile-first;
- accessible;
- SEO-ready;
- fast;
- maintainable;
- content-driven;
- reduced-motion compatible;
- production-build clean.

## Product boundaries
Do not add without explicit approval:
- authentication;
- dashboards;
- CMS;
- database;
- AI assistant;
- chatbot;
- payments;
- booking;
- client portal;
- blog;
- multilingual support;
- new service categories.

## Content integrity
Never publish:
- fake client counts;
- fake results;
- fake testimonials;
- unverified addresses;
- unapproved titles;
- fabricated partnerships;
- guarantees;
- misleading `360` naming.

## Performance objectives
- no avoidable layout shift;
- fast first render;
- optimized images;
- controlled JavaScript;
- no heavy WebGL by default;
- no decorative multi-megabyte hero video when SVG is sufficient;
- no animation that blocks content.

## Accessibility objectives
- semantic landmarks;
- one clear H1;
- correct heading hierarchy;
- keyboard navigation;
- visible focus;
- accessible mobile menu;
- reduced motion;
- proper form labels and errors;
- sufficient contrast;
- touch-friendly controls.

## Success metrics
The website succeeds when:
- Via-We is understood in five seconds;
- services are clear;
- the connected-system idea is memorable;
- relevant visitors start conversations;
- the site can be shown confidently to future clients;
- mobile and reduced motion are first-class;
- no unsupported content is published.

## Delivery phases
1. Foundation
2. Homepage
3. Brand experience
4. Service pages
5. Proof and conversion
6. Production QA and launch

## Definition of done
Version 1 is ready only when:
- public business scope is approved;
- active service pages are complete;
- homepage story is coherent;
- contact paths work;
- no fake proof remains;
- mobile and reduced motion are complete;
- lint, typecheck, tests, and build pass;
- content is approved;
- deployment is repeatable.
