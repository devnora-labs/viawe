# Via-We Content System

## Purpose
This document controls what the website may say. It prevents inconsistent copy, unsupported claims, generic AI language, and service drift.

## Approved identity
**Company:** VIA-WE Services Pvt. Ltd.
**Tagline:** YOUR DREAMS OUR AIM
**Working category:** Business Growth & Execution Company

**Core positioning**

> Via-We connects business setup, branding and digital marketing, websites and mobile applications, and franchise expansion through one coordinated execution system.

## Active service content

### Business Setup
Safe themes:
- planning;
- coordination;
- setup support;
- launch preparation;
- partner coordination.

Do not claim legal, accounting, tax, registration, or compliance delivery unless confirmed.

### Branding & Digital Marketing
Safe themes:
- positioning;
- brand identity;
- content planning;
- social media;
- paid media;
- creative production;
- campaign reporting and optimization.

Do not guarantee leads, revenue, rankings, followers, or reach.

### Website Development
Safe themes:
- business websites;
- responsive development;
- web applications;
- performance;
- SEO foundations;
- maintainable systems;
- scoped integrations.

### Mobile App Development
Safe themes:
- product planning;
- mobile UI;
- app development;
- API integration;
- testing;
- release support.

Do not promise platforms or technologies that are not confirmed per project.

### Franchise Solutions
Safe themes:
- model planning;
- brand readiness;
- expansion strategy;
- lead coordination;
- launch support;
- marketing support.

Do not make legal, investment-return, or guaranteed-success claims.

## Voice rules
Write:
- short sentences;
- plain English;
- direct verbs;
- practical explanations;
- confident but honest copy.

Avoid:
- buzzwords;
- excessive adjectives;
- vague inspiration;
- unsupported superlatives;
- language that sounds generated.

Bad:
> We deliver seamless, innovative solutions that elevate your business.

Better:
> We plan the work, connect the right service, and coordinate execution from one place.

## CTA vocabulary
Preferred:
- Start a conversation
- Explore our services
- Discuss your project
- Talk to Via-We
- Start on WhatsApp
- View the process

Avoid:
- Submit
- Click here
- Unlock growth
- Elevate now
- Learn more everywhere

## Proof rules
A public claim requires:
- source;
- date;
- owner approval;
- public-use approval;
- exact wording.

Never invent:
- client counts;
- revenue results;
- performance metrics;
- awards;
- ratings;
- testimonials.

## Photography captions
Good:
> Campaign planning session at the Via-We office.

Bad:
> Where innovation meets excellence.

## Contact content
- WhatsApp is the primary conversion path.
- Instagram may be shown after the official URL is supplied.
- Remove LinkedIn and YouTube unless official active profiles are approved.
- Publish only verified contact information.

## SEO content rules
Each route needs:
- unique title;
- useful description;
- one primary intent;
- structured headings;
- relevant service language;
- local context only when verified.

No keyword stuffing or thin pages.

## Placeholder policy
Internal markers only:
```text
[NEEDS COPY]
[NEEDS EVIDENCE]
[NEEDS OWNER DECISION]
[NEEDS ASSET]
```

These must never ship publicly.

## Content approval checklist
1. Is it true?
2. Is it supported?
3. Is it clear?
4. Does it help the visitor?
5. Is the service active?
6. Is the CTA consistent?
7. Is it free from generic AI language?
8. Is private data absent?
9. Is the legal meaning safe?
10. Is owner approval recorded?
