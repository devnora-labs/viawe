# 07 · Conversion Architecture

**Conversion is choreographed trust, not CTA placement.** The page sells a relationship, so the mechanics are built around *when a visitor becomes able to decide* — mapped beat by beat against the emotional journey (02).

## The decision-point map

| Chapter | Visitor should… | Mechanism | Action available |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arrive I | **Pause.** Feel stillness; read eleven words | Overture holds attention; zero asks | none — asking here is cheap |
| Arrive II | **Read, then choose a path** | One scope sentence; two honest exits | Primary "Start a conversation" (for hot referral traffic) · quiet "See what we do" |
| The System | **Scroll and locate themselves** | Spine + stations; bridges answer "why in this order" | — (mid-chapter CTAs would turn the argument into a pitch) |
| System terminus | **First decide-or-continue fork** | The road pauses; a breath | One quiet "Start the conversation" — for visitors whose question is already answered |
| The Belief | **Trust** | Conviction copy; the founder's world | none — belief chapters that sell undermine themselves |
| The Road | **Project themselves** (the peak) | Immersion; operational specificity | none *during*; the spell is the value |
| Road exit | **Carry readiness forward** | The pill quietly gains its conversation affordance | Persistent, one tap, from here to the end of the page |
| The Rhythm | **De-risk** | Week-one specificity (group, plan, report) | — (settling, not selling) |
| Begin | **Decide** | Tagline lands post-demonstration; the line resolves at the action | **Binary:** WhatsApp (dominant) · Call (secondary) |
| Sign-off | **Verify** | Verbatim address, legal name, socials, email | Utility contacts for the methodical |

**Total asks on the page: three** (Arrive II, terminus, Begin) plus the post-Road pill. Every other chapter deliberately asks for nothing — restraint is what makes the three asks credible.

## Why WhatsApp is primary (and how it's engineered)

The truth document's operating workflow begins with a client WhatsApp group; this audience *lives* there — a form is friction, WhatsApp is home.

- **Pre-filled opener:** the link carries "Hi Via-We — I'd like to talk about my business." The blank-message hesitation (staring at an empty chat wondering what to type) is the funnel's largest silent leak, and this removes it for one line of URL. The single cheapest conversion fix on the site.
- **Own context:** opens in a new context; the page — their reference material during the conversation — stays alive behind it.
- **Call as second door:** a real portion of this audience decides by voice; `tel:` stays one tap away, visually subordinate.
- **Email demoted to footer:** three parallel actions made the close ternary and weaker; email is for the methodical, and the methodical read footers.

## Copy rules at decision points

- Verbs first, outcomes named: "Start a conversation" — never "Submit," "Learn more," "Get in touch."
- The promise beside the ask lowers the cost of asking: *"We'll tell you what's realistic before you commit to anything"* — stays.
- **No urgency mechanics, ever:** no "limited slots," no countdowns, no exclamation marks. Manufactured pressure re-triggers the wariness this whole page exists to lower (02), and it is beneath the brand.
- **No unverified promises:** response-time commitments ("we reply within 2 hours") render only after Vamshi/Vijay confirm one that operations can keep. A promise we can't keep is a claim the governance blocks.

## Trust mechanics (the conversion substrate)

Conversion on this page is downstream of trust, and trust is built from what we *can* show under policy:

1. **Specificity as proof:** week-one artifacts (the group, the plan before production, the readable report) — observable process replacing blocked testimonials.
2. **Verified facts rendered verbatim:** real address, real phone, legal lockup — the boring credibility layer, kept flawless.
3. **The share surface:** an OG image (mark + tagline on ink) and LocalBusiness structured data — because the first impression often happens *inside WhatsApp as a link preview*, before the page is ever opened. The preview is a conversion surface and is designed as one.
4. **The absence of dark patterns as a visible trait:** no popups, no exit-intent, no chat widget imitating a human. The visitor notices what the page *doesn't* do to them.

## Measurement (so the architecture can learn)

- UTM discipline per the truth document's landing-page rules; the three asks carry distinct tracking so their relative pull is known.
- The funnel's observable stages: arrival → terminus reached → road completed → pill used vs. Begin used → conversation opened. Scroll-depth at the terminus and Road-exit are the two health metrics of the storytelling itself.
- **Deferred, on the record:** an on-page capture fallback (short form with validation, consent, CRM handoff per the landing-page model in the truth doc) for WhatsApp-reluctant visitors — Phase 4, because done badly it damages more trust than it captures. The leak is known, scheduled, and accepted for now.
