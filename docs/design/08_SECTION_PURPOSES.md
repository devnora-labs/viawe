# 08 · Section Purposes — the IA contract

**The quick-reference sheet.** One card per chapter: its single job, its single message, what it must contain, what it must never contain. The full treatment lives in 01; this is the contract every future builder (human or agent) checks before touching a section. If a change violates a card, the change is wrong — or this contract gets amended first, deliberately.

---

### Chrome (header · pill · menu)
- **Job:** carry the visitor; never perform.
- **Single message:** *you're accompanied.*
- **Must contain:** mark + wordmark at rest → pill on scroll; three destinations; verified contact routes in menu; post-Road conversation affordance.
- **Must never contain:** more than three nav items · promotional copy · the tagline · a second persistent object.
- **Depends on:** current-chapter state; pill legibility on every ground.
- **Success:** never remembered; never lost.

### Arrive I — the overture
- **Job:** state the thesis in five seconds — calm, serious, connected.
- **Single message:** *we build, brand & grow businesses.*
- **Must contain:** eyebrow (category) · three-beat headline · the signature crossing at full voice.
- **Must never contain:** a CTA · a photograph · more than eighteen words · a loader.
- **Depends on:** overture choreography (04); charged right-half composition.
- **Success:** the visitor scrolls, calmer than they arrived.

### Arrive II — the invitation
- **Job:** name the scope; open the two honest exits.
- **Single message:** *one coordinated team does all of it.*
- **Must contain:** the verified scope sentence · primary + quiet CTA · one human frame (slot: team/office).
- **Must never contain:** the night-megacity plate · a second paragraph · dark imagery (the dark belongs to The Road).
- **Success:** hot traffic converts; everyone else continues informed.

### The System — ecosystem
- **Job:** make five pillars legible as one road; route every visitor.
- **Single message:** *every stage feeds the next.*
- **Must contain:** spine + five stations in feeding order · truthful scope rows · bridges in the narrative voice · one pattern-break media station (Brand & Market) · deepening ground · terminus + quiet CTA.
- **Must never contain:** service *cards* · alphabetical or importance ordering · invented capabilities · five identical stanzas · a desktop-only spine.
- **Success:** the visitor can name where on the road they are; scroll-depth holds through station five.

### The Belief — ex-Partner
- **Job:** give the company a soul; close the "who" gap.
- **Single message:** *your dream is taken personally; you explain it once.*
- **Must contain:** eyebrow · short manifesto set large · one human + one process frame.
- **Must never contain:** the vendor-gap argument (that's The System's) · titles or bios for anyone (ungated) · values-poster abstractions · a CTA.
- **Success:** one quotable sentence survives into a WhatsApp retelling.

### The Road — pathway
- **Job:** the emotional peak; the visitor projects their business onto the journey.
- **Single message:** *taken the whole way.*
- **Must contain:** threshold entry · six stages, one at a time, continuously choreographed · operationally concrete lines · mesh field · clean exit.
- **Must never contain:** CTAs inside the pin · hard cuts between stages · adjectives where operations should be ("world-class execution") · a seventh stage without a truth-document basis.
- **Success:** the visitor reaches the exit; the pill affordance meets formed readiness.

### The Rhythm — approach
- **Job:** de-risk; show the real operating cadence as proof-of-method.
- **Single message:** *no mystery — here is week one.*
- **Must contain:** band photograph · four steps in operational language, each traceable to the truth document's workflow.
- **Must never contain:** process-jargon diagrams · fake precision ("our 7-step proprietary framework") · claims about results.
- **Success:** the four facts survive retelling: one conversation, one group, plan before production, readable report.

### Begin — closing
- **Job:** collect the decision.
- **Single message:** *the road ends at a conversation.*
- **Must contain:** the tagline's single styled appearance · one headline · one promise sentence · WhatsApp dominant (pre-filled, own context) · call secondary · the line's resolution node.
- **Must never contain:** three parallel actions · email in the action row · urgency mechanics · new information of any kind.
- **Success:** conversations open; the page stays alive behind them.

### Sign-off — footer
- **Job:** permanence; the brand's signature.
- **Single message:** *a real company, at a real address, behind everything above.*
- **Must contain:** the mark at generous scale (the page's one full logo presentation) · verbatim address · verified contact routes incl. email · socials · legal line.
- **Must never contain:** unverified spellings or wordings · a second tagline rendering (if Begin holds it) · marketing copy.
- **Success:** the last image seen is the mark, now understood.

---

## Cross-section laws

1. **One message per chapter.** Content serving another chapter's message moves there — this is how the Phase 2 duplicate-argument bug becomes structurally impossible.
2. **Facts flow only from the truth document and intake.** Gated items (founder imagery, titles, client anything, statistics, quotes, response-time promises) have styles and slots ready but render nothing until cleared.
3. **The narrative order is fixed:** Arrive → System → Belief → Road → Rhythm → Begin → Sign-off. Reordering is a brand decision, not a layout decision.
4. **Every chapter ships its mobile and reduced-motion form with the same message** — verified, not assumed.
5. **Asks live only where the map (07) puts them.** A CTA added anywhere else is a contract violation, whatever the conversion argument of the week.
