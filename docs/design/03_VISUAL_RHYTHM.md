# 03 · Visual Rhythm

**The page as a score.** Tone, density, tempo, and temperature mapped end to end, so no chapter ever reads as "another section."

## The full map

```
CHAPTER            GROUND                  DENSITY      TEMPO        LINE STATE
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Arrive I           paper (light)           near-empty   held         DRAWING → JOINS (the crossing)
Arrive II          paper                   sparse       slow         holds beneath, hands down
                                        ── breath ──
The System         paper → paper-deep      building     measured     DRAWING (spine), joins ×5
  station 2*       (pattern break: media)  pulse        syncopation  —
  terminus         paper-deep              sparse       pause        HANDS OFF ("what that road feels like—")
                                        ── breath ──
The Belief         paper-deep              sparse/large still        HOLDS (rules only)
                                        ── descent ──
The Road           ink (dark)              one-at-a-time pinned      DIVES, travels the chapter
                                        ── surfacing ──
The Rhythm         paper (light)           ordered      calm         HOLDS (ledger rules)
                                        ── gather ──
Begin              ink (dark)              near-empty   held         ARRIVES → RESOLVES (final node)
Sign-off           ink                     utility      still        AT REST (the mark itself)
```

*Reading the columns:* **Ground** is temperature and tone. **Density** is how much competes per viewport. **Tempo** is how fast the page asks you to move (pinning = slowest). **Line state** is the design language's posture per chapter (see 04 for the grammar).

## The shape in one sentence

**Light–open → light–systematic (deepening) → mid–still → dark–immersive → light–ordered → dark–held.**
Two dark moments only, and they are different sizes: The Road is the *long* dark (immersion), Begin is the *short* dark (decision). Light between them is what makes both read.

## Rhythm principles

1. **Alternation is the pulse.** Empty↔dense, light↔dark, moving↔still. No two consecutive chapters share all three values — that is the precise definition of "another section," and it is checked chapter by chapter above.
2. **The deepening is the plot.** Paper darkens across The System's stations, so the visitor *feels* the road heading somewhere before the dark chapter arrives. The page's tonal arc mirrors its narrative arc: the descent is earned, not sprung.
3. **Breaths are designed, not leftover.** The marked breaths are real vertical pauses (bay-scale) with nothing in them but ground and, sometimes, the line handing off. Whitespace is pacing; it is specified, never "remaining space."
4. **One syncopation per movement.** The System's media station (Brand & Market) breaks its own meter once — which is what makes the meter audible. One break; a second would destroy the system it reveals.
5. **Peaks are rationed.** The page has exactly two spectacle moments (the overture; the dark chapter) and one held moment (Begin). Everything else is deliberately mezzo — three forte marks in a score is drama; ten is noise.
6. **Images are beats, not wallpaper.** Photography appears at rhythm points: one human frame per light chapter, one plate per road stage, one band before The Rhythm. Never two adjacent image-led viewports in the light chapters.
7. **Mobile compresses lengths, never removes beats.** Chapters shorten; the alternation, breaths, deepening, and both darks survive at 390px. The rhythm is the story; the story ships on phones.

## Density budget (enforceable)

Per viewport, at any scroll position: **one primary element** (headline, station, stage, or action), at most one supporting element, and ground. If a viewport screenshot shows three competing voices, the layout is over budget — this is the review test used before any section ships.

## Anti-patterns (rejected on sight)

- Equal-height section slabs with uniform padding ("template breathing").
- A third dark section, or opening dark (kills the descent's meaning — and the logo cannot live on dark raster).
- Background tints alternating per section (zebra rhythm is decoration, not pacing).
- Parallax on everything (depth is spent where the story needs it: hero signature, road plates, closing backdrop).
- Any viewport where two chapters are simultaneously arguing (transitions exist so chapters never overlap voices).
