# 05 · Photography System

**Photography is the evidence layer.** Type and motion say "we have taste"; photographs say "we exist." Today every frame testifies for someone else's company — this system replaces that with a world only Via-We can photograph, and defines the interim standard until the camera does its work.

## The foundation (binding for every category)

- **World:** Via-We's actual world — the Vijayawada office, real desks, real whiteboards, real shoots, real streets. If a frame could hang on any agency's site, it fails.
- **Moment:** candid over posed; work **mid-motion** — marker touching whiteboard, hands on keyboard, a laugh mid-meeting. Nobody looks at the camera except the founder category. No stock rituals: no handshakes, no thumbs-up, no team-arms-crossed.
- **Lens & angle:** human perspective, 35–50mm equivalent, eye height or slightly below. No drone establishing shots, no fisheye, no top-down "flatlay culture."
- **Light:** honest daylight and practical office light; shadows allowed to be shadows. No HDR crunch, no ring-light flatness.
- **Grade:** cool shadows leaning toward ink-blue, honest warm skin, whites near paper. One grade across all categories — the grade *is* brand consistency. No orange-teal wash, no faded-film filters.
- **Composition:** negative space falls toward the text side of the layout the frame serves; motion vectors and eye-lines point **into** the page (echoing the curves' direction, lower-left toward upper-right where possible).
- **Clothing:** what people actually wear to this office — clean, unstyled, no wardrobe uniform, no costume "startup casual."
- **Purpose rule:** every frame answers *"what does this prove?"* If the answer is "nothing, it looks nice," the frame is cut. There are no decorative images in this system.

## Interim standard (until the shoot)

Stock is a **placeholder, not a face** — so it must at minimum rhyme with the company:

1. Indian business contexts wherever available; South Indian office/street texture preferred.
2. Same grade, ratio, and composition rules as the final system — interim frames are hung on the identical hooks.
3. Alt text stays honest: describes what is pictured; never captions a stand-in as Via-We's own. (This rule is already law in the media registry — it remains law.)
4. The two GATED categories (founder, clients) ship with neutral stand-ins from *meetings*/*office* — never a stock "CEO portrait," which would be a lie in a suit.

## The shoot (one day, Vijayawada — the deliverable that retires this debt)

Morning: office, team, developers, whiteboards, meetings (natural work, photographer embedded, no direction beyond "keep working").
Midday: brand-sessions, project-planning, website-reviews (desks, hands, screens with approved work).
Afternoon: founder session (30 min, window light, three setups), workshops if scheduled.
Golden hour: outdoor — streets, storefronts, the city that anchors "Operating from Vijayawada."
Ten good frames fill every ungated slot; the media registry swap requires **zero code changes** — that is what the slot system was built for.

## Governance

- Founder/leadership publication: requires Vijay's recorded approval (photo exists per intake; approval does not).
- Client-visible frames: written permission, per the case-study rules in the truth document.
- No frame ever fakes: no mocked client logos on screens, no staged "awards," no borrowed offices.
- Every published frame gets a registry entry with slot, ratio, and honest alt text — the registry is the single source of imagery truth.
